# Three envelopes and a burst cap replace the single budget line in the Forge

The power budget was one line (1100 points) over everything a forged champion is, and
ADR 0012 left a hole open on purpose: a creator who lightens the kit under roughly half
the line reaches every stat rail at once, because the rails together (405 for base
stats, 157 for growth) sit inside what a bare kit leaves. Playtest confirmed the second
hole the same day: one damage effect at the rail on a twenty second cooldown costs 158
points and deals 496 at level 1 against 540 health, because the budget prices damage
per second of availability and never the size of one hit. We decided two things.

The power budget is now the sum of three envelopes that never trade points with one
another: base stats 190, growth 90, the passive and four spells 820. Each is sized to
the roster's maximum in that category with no margin (ashvyn's stats, fenn's growth,
dain's kit), pinned by tests, so a champion with every envelope full is the roster's
equal and never its better, and no set of polygon vertices reaches every rail. On top
sits a burst cap: what one cast deals to a single target at rank 1, measured at the
attack rail (80), no AP, against the roster's lowest level-1 health (540), with ticked
damage counted for two seconds (what a slow or a root pins), may not exceed half that
health for a basic spell, three quarters for the ultimate, nine tenths for the three
basics together. Those fractions sit just above the roster measured the same way
(fenn's W at 48 percent, ashvyn's R at 75, fenn's basics at 85), so every roster twin
still validates and the roster stays the definition of "fits".

Considered and rejected: one line with per-category ceilings looser than the roster
(keeps a cross-category trade no roster champion makes, and lets a light kit buy more
stats than any roster champion has); tighter burst fractions (35, 70 and 85 percent
would reject fenn, torv and vesk); measuring burst with the champion's own attack
(precise, but it couples the polygon to the dial: raising attack would pull a spell
back, and the envelopes stop being independent).

Consequences. The fresh draft is retuned under every cap (its zone alone measured 57
percent). A sealed champion that no longer validates leaves the Forge queue, which
already revalidates, and is flagged to its owner to reforge. The kit conversation fits
proposals to the kit envelope, holds any spell a burst cap stops, and lets the others
take the room. The polygon and the dial say which line stopped them. And because the
envelopes are dictated by single champions today, the roster's kits will be raised
toward the kit envelope in a separate pass (damage and heal amounts only, crowd control
untouched, bot matches before and after), the deliberate opposite of lowering dain.
