# Recorded sound bank

Every file here is rendered by `scripts/build_sfx.mjs` from recordings
published under CC0 (public domain dedication): layered, trimmed,
pitched, filtered and mixed into the game's own sounds. The source
packs, all CC0:

- Kenney (kenney.nl): Impact Sounds, RPG Audio, Sci-Fi Sounds, Digital Audio.
- rubberduck (opengameart.org): 80 CC0 RPG SFX, 100 CC0 SFX #2.
- artisticdude (opengameart.org): Swishes Sound Pack, Freeze Spell, RPG Sound Pack.
- starninjas (opengameart.org): 20 Sword Sound Effects (Attacks and Clashes).
- vehicle (opengameart.org): Fantasy Sound Effects (tinysized SFX).
- someoneman (opengameart.org): Cure Magic.
- jaggedstone (opengameart.org): Magic Spell SFX.
- qubodup (opengameart.org): Energy Drain.
- pyranostudios (opengameart.org): Air whoosh.
- kurt (opengameart.org): Gunshots.
- tabasco (opengameart.org): Gunshot Sounds.
- qubodup (opengameart.org): Earth element magic spell, Sand spell, Ghost breath.
- Almitory (opengameart.org): Fantasy magic spell.
- bart (opengameart.org): Ice spells.
- Ogrebane (opengameart.org): Teleport Spell.
- themightyglider (opengameart.org): Catching fire.
- pauliuw (opengameart.org): Flight or spell sound.
- Spring Spring (opengameart.org): Magic Words + Healing Sound Effect.
- fvcalderan (opengameart.org): Gravity Inverter.
- Sorth (opengameart.org): Ghost Voice Pack.
- Ecrivain (opengameart.org): Random SFX.

CC0 asks for no attribution; these credits are kept out of respect for
the recordists. docs/design/sound.md explains how the bank is built and
which game sound each recording feeds.
